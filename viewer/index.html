How to participate

This page is a read-only public board showing every wish-list idea submitted by LAAPOA members.

To submit your own idea: open the submission form at the link below before the deadline. Submissions are anonymous by default, with an option to identify yourself if you want a follow-up.

To 👍 or 👎 an idea: open the LAAPOA membership app and go to the Announcements tab. Reactions are tracked per member and can be changed at any time.
